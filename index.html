Who We Are

An independent foundation with a very simple idea: people come first.

Kulang Foundation was registered in South Sudan in 2017 under the Government of South Sudan. We are an independent, non-profit making organisation. That means every shilling we receive goes into the work, and every project we run belongs to the community that asked for it.

We work in three areas that keep coming back to the same thing: peace, youth, and dignity. Through sport. Through community dialogue. Through standing with the people who have been pushed to the margins and reminding them that they are seen.

Founder & Chairman

Hon. Gatwech Peter Kulang

Humanitarian. Diplomat. Author. Community builder. Twenty years of walking alongside the people of South Sudan.

Chairman, Kulang Foundation

Hon. Gatwech Peter Kulang

Author

"Integrating Diplomacy in Search of Humanity"

Faith

Devoted member of Christian Temple Church (CTC) of South Sudan

Education

Cavendish University Uganda · Victoria University Melbourne · Holmesglen Institute

"Relief is not just about distributing aid. It is about restoring dignity, rebuilding trust, and reigniting hope."

Gatwech Peter Kulang has spent more than two decades in humanitarian and community development work. He has walked the dusty roads of displacement camps in South Sudan and sat in the high-level negotiation rooms where policy is shaped. He knows this work from the ground up, and he brings that to Kulang Foundation every single day.

Leadership in practice

In December 2025, Hon. Kulang joined H.E. Governor Jacob Dollar Rut on a peace tour across Upper Nile State. In August 2026, he attended the Juba Inter Nuer Churches Youth Ministry Conference in Mangateen. Wherever there is work to be done for peace and youth, he shows up.

What We Do

Three pillars. One purpose.

Sport, peace, and community. Everything we do runs through these three. No shortcuts, no noise.

Sport

Sport and Talent

Through the Kulang Sports Academy and sponsored tournaments, we give young people a place to play, to grow, and to be seen. Football is the door. Discipline, teamwork and hope are what comes through it.

  • Under-20 football teams
  • Girls' football tournaments
  • Peace tournaments across the region
Sports Academy
Peace

Peace and Unity

We join peace tours, support inter-community gatherings, and use sport as a bridge between groups that have been divided. When young people play together, old lines start to fade.

  • Peace tours with state leadership
  • Inter-community sports events
  • Dialogue through shared activities

Kulang Sports Academy

Where talent meets
discipline.

The Kulang Sports Academy is where it all happens. Under-20 football teams. Girls' tournaments. Training sessions that build character as much as skill. Our young athletes wear the badge with pride, and they have earned it.

The academy has produced winning teams, and it has produced something more important: young people who show up, work hard, and respect one another.
