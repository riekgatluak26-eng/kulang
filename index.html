Who We Are

A community and faith-based organisation with one simple belief: people come first.

Kulang Foundation Inc. (KFI) exists and operates as a community and faith-based organisation inspired by the desire to support humanity and the spirit of giving back to society, as inscribed in Christian values. Members of the Kulang family established the Foundation as a platform for sharing the dreams and inspiration of Elder Kulang and for supporting less privileged and vulnerable individuals and communities to realise their potentials.

The Foundation was established in 2017 as an appreciation and recognition from Elder Peter Kulang and his sons, family members, friends and well-wishers around the world who support the vision of bridging the educational gap left by civil war — a war that rendered most of our youthful citizens with limited educational opportunities.

KFI is an independent, non-profit making organisation registered in South Sudan in 2017 under the Government of South Sudan, Non-Governmental Organization Act.

Our Vision

A nation in which all people have pathways to basic services and opportunities to maximise their potentials — irrespective of their social status.

Our Mission

To partner with others to improve the quality of life of vulnerable and underprivileged people in South Sudan through provision of education opportunities and other basic services.

Our Ultimate Aim

To assist the less privileged through the glorification of God by equipping young men and women with life skills for eternity — especially nurturing youngsters in a growing relationship with God through Christ. We seek to promote all aspects of personal growth and to help youth develop their abilities to the full, attaining a rich and meaningful life dedicated to the service of God and humanity.

Gatwech Peter Kulang has spent more than two decades in humanitarian and community development work. Following years of experience as a welfare and community development worker with a number of organisations — several of which he helped to form — he was encouraged by friends and partners around the world to keep working on behalf of the underprivileged people of South Sudan.

He worked closely with SIL Sudan as a Publisher, trained by his supervisor David Burke to produce premier books and church materials. Since 1998 he has maintained a global circle of friends and supporters who stand with the Foundation today.

Kulang Sports & Education Academy

Where talent meets
discipline.

The Kulang Sports and Education Academy (KSEA) is one of the Foundation's flagship projects in South Sudan. It is officially registered as a local Academy by the State Ministry of Youth and Sports in Central Equatoria, and is a member of the South Sudan Football Association (SSFA) and the South Sudan Basketball Association (SSBA).

There is no other structured sports and education academy in the entire country offering young people aged 5–18 the opportunity to learn, improve and perfect their talents while simultaneously quenching their educational needs.

Child Hope Centre

A rehabilitation centre within the CTC compound in Mangateen, established under the Children Ministry Fellowship program to help orphans and vulnerable children (OVC) drawn from the less fortunate and street children of Juba.
